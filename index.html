📖 How to Play & Game Rules ▼
  1. The system secretly generates a random 4-digit number with unique digits (e.g., 5832).
  2. The first digit is never zero (0).
  3. Your goal is to deduce the exact secret number in as few attempts as possible.
  4. After entering a guess, you receive two critical clues:
    • 🟢 Correct Digits: How many digits in your guess exist anywhere in the secret number.
    • 🔵 Correct Locations: How many digits are both existing and in the exact correct spot.
  5. Use deduction and process of elimination to refine your next guess!
💡 Example Walkthrough:
